# Documentation & Reporting

## Overview
This module covers the importance of documentation and reporting in cybersecurity. It emphasizes the need for thorough documentation of findings, actions taken, and any other relevant information during a penetration test or security assessment. Proper documentation and reporting are crucial for maintaining a clear record of activities, ensuring compliance, and facilitating the sharing of knowledge within a team or organization.
