# smb

## Mounting VHD via SMB Share
- `mkdir smbmount`
- `mount -t cifs //10.10.14.10/david -o user=david smbmount`

## Nmap Enumeration
- `sudo nmap 10.10.14.10 -sV -sC -p139,445`

## smbmap
- `smbmap -H 10.10.14.10`
- `smbmap -u david -p gRzX7YbeTcDG7 -H 10.10.14.10`
- `smbmap -H 10.10.14.10 -u anonymous -R`

## smbclient
- `smbclient -N -L //10.10.14.10`
- `smbclient -L //10.10.14.10/SHARENAME`
- `smbclient -N -L //10.10.14.10/SHARENAME`
- `smbclient -U administrator //10.10.14.10/ADMIN$`
- `smbclient -U david //10.10.14.10/david`
- `smbclient //10.10.14.10/GGJ -U
