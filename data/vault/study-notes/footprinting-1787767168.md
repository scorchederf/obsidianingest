# Footprinting

## Introduction
Footprinting is the first step in the ethical hacking process. It involves gathering information about the target organization or system to understand its structure, vulnerabilities, and potential attack vectors. This can be done through various means such as public sources, social engineering, and network scanning.

## Public Sources
Information can be gathered from public sources such as the company's website, social media, and other publicly available information. This can include the company's mission statement, product offerings, and contact information. Additionally, information can be gathered from the target's domain name, IP addresses, and other publicly available data.

## Social Engineering
Social engineering involves manipulating people into divulging confidential information. This can be done through phishing attacks, pretexting, and other methods. It is important to use social engineering techniques responsibly and ethically.

## Network Scanning
Network scanning involves using tools such as Nmap to scan the target network for open ports, services, and vulnerabilities. This can be done to gather information about the target's network topology and potential attack vectors.
