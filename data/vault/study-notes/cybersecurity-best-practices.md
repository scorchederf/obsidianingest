# Cybersecurity Best Practices

## Protect Your Devices
Keep your software, operating system and browser up to date
Run a reputable antivirus product
Only download applications from Google Play or the App Store and never from a third-party app store
Make backups in case of data loss.

## Secure Your Account Logins
Don’t reuse the same or similar passwords or known details such as birthdates, children’s or pet’s names or favourite places
Use a password manager - bitwarden
Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)

## Think Before You Click or Share
Don’t click on links or open attachments in unsolicited emails or text messages
Report unsolicited emails as spam or junk and delete them rather than clicking the unsubscribe button.
Be cautious about sharing Personally Identifiable Information (PII) over the phone, in email or via text message, especially if you did not initiate the contact
Limit how much information you share on social media and lock down the privacy settings on your accounts.
Only give applications the permissions they really need.

## Cybersecurity on the Go
Avoid using public Wi-Fi hotspots like the ones at coffee shops, airports, hotels, etc. Instead, stick to the mobile network and create a personal Wi-Fi hotspot with your phone
Don’t use publicly available charging cords or USB ports with your devices.

## At Home
Change the default passwords for items like routers, wifi and smart home technologies, such as speakers and virtual assistants

## Social Media
Be wary of accepting new friends or followers
Limit what you post and share
Be wary when using tags or commenting on posts – tagging social media photos and posts could reveal your location, while tagging companies, comments made on public posts and hashtags can generally be seen by the public, even if you have a private account
