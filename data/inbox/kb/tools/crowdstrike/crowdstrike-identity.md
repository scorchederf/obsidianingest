## IDP 170: Falcon Identity Protection Fundamentals
- IDP (Identity Protection) offers real-time threat detection and response, leveraging AI and behavioral analytics to identify and mitigate identity-related threats.
- **IT**
	- Know how many accounts are active or stale, how many unmanaged endpoints exist in the domain, and in general gain better understanding of the domain attack surface.
	- Identify unused servers or most access servers by analyzing authentication footprint.
	- Detect use of weak authentication protocols such as NTLM, v1 or unencrypted of LDAP.
	- Misconfigurations of the domain.
- **Identity Access Management (IAM)**
	- Build user behavioral profiles that can help understand the access patterns for human and programmatic accounts.
	- Detect anomalous user behavior.
	- Identify user segments such as how many are human? How many are programmatic? How many are privileged or have stealthy privileges?
	- Apply a policy to prevent some of the issues continuously.
